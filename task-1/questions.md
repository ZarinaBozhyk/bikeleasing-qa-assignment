# Clarification questions

1. What should happen when the claim does not exist in the system?

2. What should happen when the user's contract is invalid?

3. What should happen when the reported bike does not match the insured bike?

4. What information identifies an existing claim and the insured bike,
   and what rules determine whether the contract is valid?

5. How are insurance coverage, submitted evidence and bike value checked?
   What happens when information is missing or a check fails?

6. What counts as a properly locked bike, and which evidence proves this?

7. How is it determined whether a damaged bike is worth repairing,
   including a case exactly at the decision boundary?

8. Which parts count as wear parts?
   How are partial payments and total-loss payments calculated?

9. Do defect and wear claims initially receive the Damage category?
   How are defect and wear identified?

10. What should happen if a claim is routed to warranty
    but warranty coverage is unavailable?

11. What should happen if the stolen bike is found before replacement
    or after the theft claim was rejected?

12. If the stolen bike is found after payment and replacement,
    must any payment be repaid, and who keeps each bike?
